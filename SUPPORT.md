## Intended Setup

1. Install the iPhone app from the eventual verified TestFlight/App Store link.
2. Sign up using Apple's native sign-in. Account access is free and requires no purchase.
3. Open the Mac installer link from the app on your own compatible Mac. Choose
   the package for Apple Silicon or Intel. Windows is not supported.
4. Install the signed/notarized package containing FocusBridge and the official
   ActivityWatch app. Grant the permissions you choose through macOS itself.
5. Send the private one-use connection link from your iPhone to the Mac using
   AirDrop, or use the manual code fallback. The link expires in ten minutes.
6. Choose productive Mac apps. On iPhone, authorize Screen Time and select the
   apps/sites to protect. Apple Health is a separate optional connection.

## Balances And Protection

The day starts with 35 active distraction minutes. Each four productive Mac minutes
earns one additional minute. Optional Move-goal rewards add one minute per completed
5%, plus 20 minutes at 100%, up to 40 minutes/day. Only measured active use spends
your balance. Every access window relocks after at most seven elapsed minutes.

Protection additions apply immediately; removals take effect the next account day.
If usage monitoring is interrupted, the app relocks rather than inventing a charge
or leaving an unmetered window open. Retry protected access or contact support if
this repeats. iOS determines callback and Live Activity delivery; a display update
is not itself proof of measured usage.

Browser productivity requires the configured frontmost-tab tracking integration;
selecting Safari as a productive application alone does not enable website earning.
The Safari Shorts filter is separate from whole-site Screen Time protection and
requires enabling the extension and granting the site permission.

## Account And Health Data

Use Settings > Delete account in the iPhone app to request account deletion. You
may need to authenticate again with Apple. Contact support if deletion fails.
Disconnect Apple Health to stop further reads/uploads; previously earned rewards
remain. Manage the underlying Health records in Apple Health and ActivityWatch's
independent local data in ActivityWatch.
